## Long-term Knowledge

### Architecture

<!-- lore:01a10245-8a45-7fd1-bf5f-c3f9950c7870 -->
* **inferMigrations count-only shifts**: Chose bounded, labeled inferred shifts over depicting observed model switches because weekly counts contain no user or session linkage. Pair only unmatched declines and rises in adjacent displayed periods; cap each ribbon by both changes and leave unmatched growth as an entry. Never bridge an absent week or infer a hidden-model self-loop. Reject inference when observed sources are supplied.

### Gotcha

<!-- lore:01a10245-8aee-7e64-85e2-7e0c9dda31b7 -->
* **Community flow-inferred filtering**: Trap: applying the personal chart’s inferred-shift logic to community counts looks consistent because both charts show weekly totals. Fix: infer crossings only from personal, gist, and mock counts, not the community chart. Community totals combine separate contributors without linking their sessions; an apparent decline and rise cannot establish a model switch, even with every opted-in cell visible.

<!-- lore:01a10245-8b81-7eee-8090-7367eaa6ed35 -->
* **Inferred ribbon nearest-edge attachments**: Trap: attaching every inferred ribbon to fixed sides of model bars looks simpler but sends switchover lines across continuity bands and other lines unnecessarily. Fix: attach each ribbon on the edge nearest the other model, stack same-side branches by the other model’s vertical position, and keep retained usage and unmatched entries between attachment bands.
